## Brand & Style

This design system draws direct inspiration from elite digital brand ateliers and contemporary creative studio interfaces (exemplified by Afternow). It merges the austere, intellectual authority of Swiss structural modernism with high-voltage interactive energy. Designed for high-stakes digital environments, live-event interactive sabotage mechanics, and editorial agency portfolios, it balances stark clarity with razor-sharp tension.

The design movement is **Brutalist Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Stark Contrast:** Architectural juxtaposition of deep voids (`#050505`) and pure bleached planes (`#FFFFFF`), avoiding muddled medium-gray compromises.
- **Electric Accentuation:** Purposeful, surgical injections of hyper-saturated acid chartreuse (`#CCFF00`) and deep cobalt violet (`#4B00E0`), signaling dynamic states, tactical triggers, and countdown urgency.
- **Architectural Wireframes:** Explicit boundary rules, fine 1px hairline dividers, razor-edged container cells, and technical grid-based information layouts.
- **Tactical Utility:** Monospaced labeling, capitalized micro-copy, tightly tracked grotesque display type, and pill-capsule badges that mimic tactical heads-up telemetry.

## Colors

The palette operates on intentional polarity. Rather than relying on layered gray scales, the system relies on high-impact binary transitions punctuated by high-chroma kinetic accents.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#050505`):** The grounding core. Governs structural typography, heavy button strokes, dark theme bases, and high-impact graphic blocks.
- **Secondary / Kinetic Chartreuse (`#CCFF00`):** The interactive catalyst. Reserved for immediate calls to action, active timer pulses, sabotage trigger confirmations, and live-event feedback loops. In light mode, text resting on chartreuse must always be `#050505`.
- **Tertiary / Deep Cobalt (`#4B00E0`):** The structural counterweight. Used for power-up mechanics, category tagging, telemetry metrics, and secondary focus states.
- **Neutral (`#737373`):** Utilitarian mid-tone strictly deployed for secondary metadata, disabled states, and mono-spaced timestamps.

### Contextual Named Colors
- **Sabotage Crimson (`#FF2A3B`):** Urgent visual disruption token for attack alerts, negative point cascades, and critical time cutoffs.
- **Signal Emerald (`#00FF85`):** Active network status, verified answers, and bonus-round indicators.
- **Hairline Dividers (`#E5E5E5` / `#222222`):** Ultra-crisp 1px boundary lines that organize dense information without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy channels architectural discipline and data-rich agency portfolios:

1. **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Modern neo-grotesque geometry featuring aggressive ink-traps and athletic stance. Tightly tracked (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) to forge cohesive editorial display blocks that command authority.
2. **Body Typography (`Work Sans`):** Clean, industrial neutral companion providing uncompromising legibility across live-action HUD overlays, long-form project case narratives, and quiz prompts.
3. **Telemetry & Meta Labels (`Space Mono`):** Fixed-pitch, technical data font used strictly in uppercase for badges, status indicators, game timer readouts, score counters, and column coordinates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by a **Modular Architectural Grid** that borrows from physical Swiss printed brochures:
- **Columns:** 12-column dynamic desktop grid transitioning to 6-column on tablet and 2-column or 1-column stack on mobile viewports.
- **Edge Hairlines & Outlines:** Grid cells often present explicit 1px hairline borders (`#E5E5E5` on light mode, `#222222` on dark mode) extending edge-to-edge, producing clean compartmentalized modular sections.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing tokens scale rigorously by base-4 intervals. Dynamic margins (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) frame major editorial breaks, while tactical HUD elements are clustered with rapid, dense margins (`space-2xs` to `space-sm`) to emulate physical instrument panels.
- **Reflow Rules:** Complex grid modules—such as agency project showcases or multiplayer sabotage rosters—collapse seamlessly into monolithic card stacks on mobile breakpoints while maintaining persistent pinned telemetry bars.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects blur-heavy soft drop shadows in favor of **Crisp Technical Elevation**:
- **Zero-Blur Hard Offset Shadows (Tactile Gaming & CTA):** Interactive controls leverage hard-edged directional offsets: `3px 3px 0px #050505` or `4px 4px 0px #CCFF00`. Buttons feel mechanical, physical, and immediate.
- **Hairline Inset Borders:** Cards and panels utilize `1px solid` crisp lines rather than floating diffuse elevations. Depth is articulated through surface color shifts (`#FFFFFF` to `#F2F2F2` or `#050505` to `#111111`).
- **High-Acuity Overlays:** Modal dialogues, active sabotage prompts, and countdown overlays utilize a semi-opaque stark backdrop (`rgba(5, 5, 5, 0.85)`) without blur, maintaining severe contrast and graphic immediacy.

## Shapes

The geometric grammar is governed by a **Sharp Mechanical Foundation** contrasted with **Pill Micro-Badges**:
- **Architectural Base (`roundedness: 0`):** Cards, visual tiles, structural grids, alert blocks, input fields, and standard container boxes feature sharp, unrounded 90-degree corners.
- **Pill Exception for Micro-Controls & Badges:** Metadata badges, status tags, pill buttons, and quick-filter switches use a continuous full radius (`rounded-full` / `9999px`). This specific dichotomy—razor-sharp content containers holding smoothly pill-shaped micro tags—is a defining signature of modern high-end editorial portfolios and game HUDs.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary Hero Action:** Sharp rectangular boundary (`rounded: 0`), black background (`#050505`), white bold text, with an offset hard shadow (`3px 3px 0px #CCFF00`). On `:hover`, translate -2px -2px with shadow extending to 5px.
- **Tactical Sabotage Button:** Electric chartreuse background (`#CCFF00`), black Space Grotesk uppercase text, accompanied by an absolute corner icon or mono tag. On `:active`, snap down to zero shadow offset for physical tactile response.
- **Editorial Pill Action:** Fully rounded pill button (`rounded-full`), black background, white text, 12px horizontal padding, with trailing mini arrow glyph `→`.

### Pill Tags & Category Badges
- Encapsulated in full pill curvature (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- Background: Translucent white or subtle light gray (`#F2F2F2`) in light mode; semi-transparent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) in dark mode.
- Typography: `Space Mono`, 11px uppercase, letter-spaced (`0.08em`).
- Active state transitions into solid chartreuse with jet-black copy.

### Cards & Modular Showcase Tiles
- Outer container framed by razor-sharp `1px solid` hairlines.
- Media sections occupy a rigid 4:3 or 16:9 ratio, terminating directly into container edges without inner padding.
- Card metadata block features stacked editorial typography: monospaced taxonomy string above a Space Grotesk headline, underlined by subtle hairline rules.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Minimalist architectural underlines or sharp rectangular boxes with 1px dark strokes.
- High-contrast focus state: 2px solid `#CCFF00` or `#4B00E0` border without glowing halos.
- Monospaced field labels positioned directly above or inset as uppercase indicators.

### Game Telemetry & HUD Components (Live Event / Sabotage)
- **Countdown Timer:** Monospaced oversized numeric font (`Space Mono`), highlighted in black pill containers with an electric chartreuse perimeter stroke.
- **Sabotage Multiplier Bar:** Segmented brutalist progress blocks (grid of 5 to 10 discrete rectangles) that light up sequentially in `#CCFF00` and flash `#FF2A3B` during hostile disruptions.
- **Player Status Rows:** Monolithic list items divided by horizontal hairlines, featuring fixed-width score counters, player avatars framed in sharp square viewports, and status indicator pills.
